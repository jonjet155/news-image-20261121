Sum up 21 November's world news in one image: one page, 50 dated stories
from Saturday 21 November 2026 — Kyiv under fire in Russia's biggest
attack of the year, the AI sell-off dragging the S&P 500 into correction,
and the ICJ's 9–6 ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the
Rohingya — plus COP31 overtime, the UK–EU reset deals, the Peru quake,
a pancreatic-cancer pill breakthrough, Gen Z protests in 25 countries,
and 36 more briefs, on a light world map with declustered pins.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` is standard library only: it copies the pre-built `entry.png`
(1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
